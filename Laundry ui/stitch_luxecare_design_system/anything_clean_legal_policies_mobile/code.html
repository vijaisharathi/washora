We collect information to provide better services to all our users. The types of information we collect include:

  • Account Information (Name, Email, Phone Number)
  • Payment Details (Processed securely via Stripe)
  • Service Location Data
  • Device and Usage Information

2. How We Use Information

We use the information we collect from all our services for the following purposes:

build Provide Services Deliver the requested cleaning and maintenance tasks.
support_agent Customer Support Resolve issues and respond to inquiries efficiently.

3. Data Sharing

We do not share your personal information with companies, organizations, or individuals outside of Anything Clean except in the following cases:

  • With your consent
  • For external processing by trusted partners
  • For legal reasons (subpoenas, court orders)

4. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. You can manage most of these settings directly from your Account Settings page.
